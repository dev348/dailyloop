Terms of Use & Privacy Policy

The Daily Loop — a reflection tool for the ISDM Bricoleur Fellowship practitioner circle.

What this app is

The Daily Loop is a small, free tool built for the fellowship's own daily and weekly reflection practice. It is not a commercial product and comes with no guarantees of uptime, accuracy, or fitness for any purpose beyond that.

What data it collects

The app only ever handles what you type into it: your daily entries (check-in, Experience, Reflect, Model, Try) and your weekly reviews. Nothing else is collected — no analytics, no tracking scripts, no third-party advertising.

Where your data lives

  • If you don't sign in: entries are stored only in your browser's local storage, on that one device. Clearing your browser data, using private/incognito mode, or switching devices will lose this data — there is no other copy.
  • If you sign in with Google: entries also sync to a special hidden folder in your own Google Drive (appDataFolder) that only this app can read. It is not visible in your normal Drive file list, and it cannot see or touch any of your other Drive files, photos, or documents.

Who can see your entries

No one but you. There is no server, no shared database, and no admin view — the people running this fellowship (including its facilitators) cannot see what you write. Your weekly circle only sees what you choose to bring and share out loud.

Google sign-in

Sign-in uses Google's own identity service. The app only ever requests the drive.appdata permission — the minimum needed to back up your entries to your own private folder. You can revoke this access at any time from your Google Account permissions page, or by using the Sign out button in the app.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual entries from within the app at any time. To remove everything, clear this site's data from your browser and, if you signed in, revoke the app's access from your Google Account — this removes the backup file from your Drive's app data folder.

Feedback

Anything sent through the app's feedback box goes only to the person maintaining this tool for the fellowship, to help fix problems or improve the practice.

Changes

Since this is a small tool built for one fellowship's use, this policy may change as the tool does. Meaningful changes will be reflected here.
